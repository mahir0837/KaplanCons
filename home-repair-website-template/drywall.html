Professional Drywall Installation & Repair in Toronto & GTA

Smooth, perfectly level drywall is the foundation of any stunning interior space. Living by our core philosophy, "Quality Not Quantity," Kaplan Construction delivers top-tier drywall hanging, patching, water damage repair, and finishing across Toronto and the Greater Toronto Area.

Why Professional Drywall Services Make All the Difference

Poorly installed or incorrectly patched drywall leaves visible seams, uneven surfaces, and fragile joints that show through paint. Whether you are building out a basement, remodeling a commercial office, or repairing damaged wallboard after a leak, precise sheetrock hanging and expert joint mudding are essential for long-term structural integrity and aesthetics.

At Kaplan Construction, our skilled technicians use fire-rated, moisture-resistant, and sound-dampening drywall materials paired with dustless sanding techniques to guarantee clean, paint-ready walls with zero disruption to your daily routine.

Our 5-Step Drywall Installation & Repair Process

  1. 1. On-Site Inspection & Framing Check
    We inspect underlying wood/metal studs to verify structural alignment, insulation, and electrical clearance before installation.
  2. 2. Precision Board Measuring & Cutting
    Drywall sheets are cut with tight tolerances around outlets, fixtures, and corners to minimize joint gaps.
  3. 3. Fastening & Securing
    Panels are securely fastened using heavy-duty drywall screws countersunk to ideal depth without tearing the paper layer.
  4. 4. Taping, Mudding & Skim Coating
    High-strength joint tape and multiple thin coats of joint compound are applied, feathering edges for an invisible seam.
  5. 5. Dust-Controlled Sanding & Prime Readiness
    Surfaces are smoothly sanded, inspected with high-intensity light for imperfections, and prepared for painting.
