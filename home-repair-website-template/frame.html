Every flawless wall, ceiling, and partition relies on a rock-solid skeleton. Guided by our motto "Quality Not Quantity," Kaplan Construction builds precise, code-compliant wood and steel stud framing for residential build-outs, basement renovations, and commercial developments across Toronto and the GTA.

Why Precision Framing is Essential for Your Building Project

Framing is the backbone of any structure. Out-of-plumb studs, improper header sizing, or uneven joists ripple into downstream issues—causing cracked drywall, sticking doors, uneven flooring, and expensive structural corrections later. Professional framing ensures perfectly square corners, level headers, and total load-bearing integrity.

At Kaplan Construction, we leverage laser-guided leveling tools, high-grade dimensional lumber, and light-gauge steel framing to construct rigid, durable wall and ceiling structures aligned strictly with Ontario Building Code regulations.

Our 5-Step Framing Execution Process

  1. 1. Architectural Review & Layout Snap
    We review structural blueprints and snap precision chalk lines onto subfloors or concrete to establish exact wall locations.
  2. 2. Material Inspection & Preparation
    Lumber and steel studs are inspected for straightness and treated appropriately for moisture or fire resistance before layout.
  3. 3. Top & Bottom Plate Fastening
    Plates are securely anchored to concrete foundations or floor systems using specialized heavy-duty mechanical or powder-actuated fasteners.
  4. 4. Stud Erection & Header Installation
    Studs are spaced precisely at 16 or 24 inches on-center, plumbed with optical lasers, and reinforced with load-rated headers.
  5. 5. Blocking, Bracing & Inspection Readiness
    Fire blocking, backing for heavy fixtures, and diagonal bracing are added so your frame is fully ready for trades and inspections.
