Professional Drywall Taping & Skim Coating in Toronto & GTA

The secret to paint perfection lies in the quality of your drywall taping and finishing. Guided by our core motto "Quality Not Quantity," Kaplan Construction offers expert taping, joint compound mudding, corner bead alignment, and Level 5 skim coating across Toronto and the Greater Toronto Area.

Why Professional Taping & Finishing Matters

Even top-grade drywall boards will show ridges, screw indentations, and visible joint seams under lighting if the taping process is rushed or poorly executed. Professional taping bridges board joints with high-tensile tape and feathered mud coats, ensuring that walls appear as a single continuous, silky-smooth plane when paint is applied.

Our experienced tapers utilize premium paper tape, fiberglass mesh, and multi-pass lightweight joint compounds combined with dust-controlled sanding technology to deliver pristine Level 4 and Level 5 surfaces for homes, luxury residences, and commercial venues.

Our Taping & Finishing Specialties

Multi-Pass Joint Taping

Seamless embedding of heavy-duty paper and mesh tape across flat joints, butt joints, and ceiling transitions.

Level 5 Skim Coating

A ultra-thin continuous coat of compound applied over the entire surface for high-gloss paint or critical lighting environments.

Skim coating damaged plaster walls, textured ceilings, and minor surface defects to restore mirror-flat surfaces.

Our 5-Step Taping & Mudding Process

  1. 1. Pre-Taping Inspection & Fastener Recessing
    We inspect all drywall sheets, driving in high screws, trimming loose paper, and pre-filling wide gaps with setting-type compound.
  2. 2. Tape Embedding (First Coat)
    Tape is embedded into a bed of joint compound along all seams, inside corners, and ceiling angles, squeezing out excess mud to eliminate air bubbles.
  3. 3. Block & Feather Coats (Second & Third Pass)
    Successive wider passes of lightweight compound are applied with finishing trowels, feathering joint edges smoothly outward.
  4. 4. Optional Level 5 Skim Coat
    For high-end finishes, a complete skim coat is sprayed or troweled across the entire wall board surface to unify porosity.
  5. 5. Dust-Controlled Sanding & Light Inspection
    Surfaces are finely sanded using vacuum-assisted sanders and inspected under directional halogen lights for a flawless primer-ready finish.

Frequently Asked Questions

Level 4 is standard for flat/eggshell paints, where joints and fasteners receive multiple mud passes. Level 5 adds a full paper-thin skim coat over the entire wall board, eliminating sheen variations caused by differing absorption between paper and joint compound under intense lighting.

Standard joint compounds usually dry in 12 to 24 hours per coat depending on humidity and ventilation. We also use quick-setting compounds when project timelines require accelerated turnarounds.
