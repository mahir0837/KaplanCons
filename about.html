About Us

Kaplan Construction helps clients design, decorate, and build with extensive experience

Founded in 2015, Kaplan Construction is a premier Toronto-based business specializing in painting services, popcorn removal, kitchen cabinet spraying, drywall patching and framing, as well as painting and varnishing.

Our guiding motto is "Quality Not Quantity," driven by an unwavering commitment to the highest levels of customer satisfaction. With over 5 years of hands-on experience across the GTA, we focus on building enduring partnerships with our clients to fuel our continuous growth.

Our diverse portfolio spans residential, commercial, and custom home projects. We pride ourselves on offering affordable pricing alongside a comprehensive suite of services, including:
