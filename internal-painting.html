Transform your home or commercial building with high-precision internal painting solutions. Guided by our motto "Quality Not Quantity," Kaplan Construction delivers smooth finishes, crisp lines, and long-lasting paint results across Toronto and the Greater Toronto Area.

Why Professional Interior Painting Matters for Your Space

A fresh coat of interior paint is one of the most effective ways to upgrade your property's aesthetic, improve lighting efficiency, and boost market value. Whether you are remodeling a single bedroom, completing a multi-story home renovation, or updating a commercial workplace, proper paint application requires skilled surface preparation and premium coating materials.

At Kaplan Construction, we combine over a decade of hands-on experience with modern painting technologies like low-VOC eco-friendly paints and high-precision sprayers to deliver flawless outcomes without chemical fumes or hassle.

Our Proven 5-Step Painting Process

  1. 1. Consultation & Color Selection
    We assist in selecting optimal paint sheens (matte, eggshell, satin, semi-gloss) and colors tailored to your interior design goals.
  2. 2. Complete Protection & Masking
    Flooring, furniture, and fixtures are fully covered using drop cloths, plastic sheeting, and precision painter’s tape.
  3. 3. Surface Preparation
    Holes and micro-cracks are filled, surfaces sanded, and high-adhesion primers applied for long-lasting durability.
  4. 4. Precision Paint Application
    Using high-grade paints (Sherwin-Williams, Benjamin Moore), our experts apply multiple coats for deep, vivid coverage.
  5. 5. Final Inspection & Clean-up
    We clean up completely and conduct a detailed walkthrough with you to ensure 100% satisfaction.
